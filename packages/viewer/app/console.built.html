Sepolia. Every button is one call to the wrapper contract — no relayer, no backend.

Mint your own

The stand-in collection is open — anybody can mint any id from 1 to 10,000, and every one of them has a real entry in the Bored Ape directory. Artwork for 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 is already on this chain, so those can be preserved straight after wrapping. Any other id will wrap fine and sit at unproven until somebody puts its bytes here.

Try to feed it bad data

Drop in any image. The page hashes it exactly the way IPFS does and shows you the name those bytes carry — a CID is the hash, so this is a sum you can do for yourself with nothing to trust. Then ask the contract, for free and without sending a transaction: it is offered another ape's artwork, which really is stored on this chain, and it refuses. Provenance was never what was being checked.
